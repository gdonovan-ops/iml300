Why Los Angeles?

This city is built on so many dreams. Sometimes dreams might get lost in the craziness of living in a new place.

Everyone has a reason for being here.

Los Angeles is a city filled with people from different places, backgrounds, and experiences.

Some come here for their careers. Some come for a fresh start. Some are searching for themselves. Everyone has a story.

Dear Los Angeles is a space for those stories to exist together. A digital wall of hopes and dreams, and a place to connect with strangers through anonymous letters.

Everyone is connected, and here is how.
